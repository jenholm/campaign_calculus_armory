## Notes

1. **Gulf War military balance:** The paper references Friedman (1998) for the Gulf War. This is a reasonable reference for coalition strategy and force composition. Additional references for Iraqi military weakness include the IWB dataset and the case study markdown file.

2. **WWI economic exhaustion:** The paper references the COW dataset and Biddle (2004) for material capability analysis. The WWI case study uses the mechanism classifier's trajectory analysis.

3. **Vietnam political exhaustion:** The paper references Huth (1996) and Bennett & Stam (2000) for the quantitative war studies tradition. The Vietnam case study's classification as "strategic exhaustion" is based on the v2 mechanism classifier's trajectory analysis.

4. **Missing references:** The paper could benefit from additional references on:
   - Gulf War air campaign specifics (e.g., Bulloch 1998, "The Mechanized Chariot")
   - WWI Western Front attrition dynamics (e.g., Prior & Wilson 2005, "The Somme")
   - Vietnam political exhaustion (e.g., Herring 2002, "America's Longest War")
   - Six Day War (e.g., Oren 2002, "Six Days of War" - already cited in case study)

5. **Corbett citation:** The paper references Clausewitz but not Corbett (Sir Julian Corbett, "Some Principles of Maritime Strategy", 1911). Corbett is the third major theorist in the Mahan/Clausewitz/Corbett trinity. Consider adding for completeness.
